Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Skill-Based Recruitment Portal. Your privacy and security are of utmost importance to us. This document outlines how we handle your data, our security measures, and your rights as a user. By using our platform, you agree to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

2. Data Protection & Security

We are committed to protecting your personal and professional data. Our platform uses advanced encryption techniques and security protocols to ensure that your personal details, skill assessments, and job applications remain secure.

We also take steps to protect against unauthorized access, data breaches, and fraudulent activities. Your privacy is our top priority, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third-party advertisers.

3. Information We Collect

When you sign up on our platform, we collect essential details such as your name, email, resume, and skills. This information helps us create the best job-matching experience for you.

Additionally, we may collect usage data to improve our platform, such as tracking job preferences and interaction patterns to enhance your experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to match you with potential employers based on your skills and qualifications. We also use your data to provide relevant job recommendations, improve our services, and ensure an efficient recruitment process.

Furthermore, we may use anonymized data to analyze industry trends and improve job-matching algorithms.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can update, modify, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings.

If you wish to request a copy of your stored data or delete your account permanently, you can reach out to our support team at support@skillrecruit.com.

6. Terms of Service

By using our platform, you agree to abide by our terms of service, which include:

  • Providing accurate and truthful information in your profile and applications.
  • Respecting the privacy and rights of other users.
  • Not engaging in fraudulent activities or misusing the platform.
  • Following ethical job application and hiring practices.

Failure to comply with these terms may result in the suspension or termination of your account.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding our privacy policy or terms of service, feel free to contact us at privacy@skillrecruit.com. Our support team is available to assist you.
