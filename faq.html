Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account? +
You can create an account by clicking the "Sign Up" button and filling out the required details.
What is the recruitment process? +
Employers post job listings with required skills. Job seekers apply, and the system matches candidates based on their skills.
What are the payment plans? +
We offer free and premium plans. Premium plans provide advanced filtering and priority listings.
How can I contact support? +
You can contact support via email at support@skillrecruit.com or helpdesk@skillrecruit.com.
How can I reset my password? +
Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.
Can employers verify candidate skills? +
Yes, we provide skill assessment tests and certification options to verify candidates' skills.
How do I update my profile information? +
You can update your profile details by navigating to your account settings and making necessary changes.
Is my data safe on this platform? +
Yes, we use advanced security measures to protect your personal and professional data.
What types of jobs are available? +
We offer jobs across multiple industries, including IT, healthcare, finance, and more, based on skill-based matching.
Can I apply for multiple jobs at once? +
Yes, you can apply for multiple job openings that match your skills and preferences.
